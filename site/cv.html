A connected view of learning

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Life Science Research Institute, KAIST. My research connects graph representation learning, semi-supervised learning, and domain adaptation with biomedical problems, including dementia subtyping, longitudinal prediction, and biomarker discovery. I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Ajou University, advised by Professor Hyunjung Shin, and previously conducted postdoctoral research at Ajou University School of Medicine.
